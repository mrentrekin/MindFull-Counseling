My commitment to you
You will be met with attention, honesty, and genuine care.

Therapy should feel safe enough for truth and purposeful enough for change. I will listen closely, speak candidly, and help you translate insight into a different way of living.

The approach

Depth without losing direction.

My work integrates evidence-based clinical approaches with careful attention to your story, relationships, values, and present life.

I

Understand the roots

I help you identify the experiences, attachment patterns, and beliefs that shaped your current responses.

II

Name the present

We clarify what is impaired now—emotionally, relationally, professionally, spiritually, or practically.

III

Practice the change

You develop realistic skills for regulation, resilience, communication, and values-based action between sessions.

About Michael

The person across from you matters.

I am the sole clinician at MindFull Counseling & Consulting. When you reach out, you reach me—and when you begin therapy, you work directly with me.

I am a Licensed Professional Counselor in Pennsylvania and a Licensed Associate Counselor in New Jersey. My experience spans private practice, school counseling, and teaching psychology at the college level.

That breadth helps me see more than a set of symptoms. I consider the larger life around them: family, development, faith, identity, vocation, and the relationships that shape how you understand yourself.

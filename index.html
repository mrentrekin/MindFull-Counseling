Reshape. Reframe. Rebuild.

Good architecture moves in order: site, structure, then the life lived inside it. My work follows the same sequence, integrating evidence-based clinical approaches with careful attention to your story, relationships, values, and present life.

I
Reshape

Survey the foundation

We identify the experiences, attachment patterns, and beliefs that poured the footings of your current responses.

II
Reframe

Redraw the plan

We clarify what is impaired now—emotionally, relationally, professionally, spiritually, or practically—and see it from a new elevation.

III
Rebuild

Raise what lasts

You practice realistic skills for regulation, resilience, communication, and values-based action until the new structure carries weight on its own.

Before you reach out.

Where is your office?

My office is at 5151 Chichester Avenue in Aston, PA 19014, in Delaware County. It is a short drive from Media, Brookhaven, Chester, Glen Mills, Garnet Valley, and Wallingford, with easy access from I-95 and Route 1.

Do you see clients from Philadelphia?

Yes. Many of my clients live in Philadelphia and meet with me by secure video, which saves the commute. Philadelphia clients who prefer in-person sessions are welcome at the Aston office, about a 30–40 minute drive from Center City.

Do you offer online therapy in Pennsylvania?

Yes. I provide telehealth counseling to adults, couples, and families anywhere in Pennsylvania through secure video.

Do you take insurance?

I am an out-of-network provider. I work with self-pay clients and some insurance options in Pennsylvania and New Jersey, and I can provide documentation you may be able to submit for out-of-network reimbursement. Reach out and I will walk you through your options.

How do I get started?

Email or call me with a few sentences about what brings you in. I respond personally, and we will set up a brief conversation to see whether I am the right fit.

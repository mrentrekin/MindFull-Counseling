The approach

Depth without losing direction.

My work integrates evidence-based clinical approaches with careful attention to your story, relationships, values, and present life.

I

Understand the roots

I help you identify the experiences, attachment patterns, and beliefs that shaped your current responses.

II

Name the present

We clarify what is impaired now—emotionally, relationally, professionally, spiritually, or practically.

III

Practice the change

You develop realistic skills for regulation, resilience, communication, and values-based action between sessions.

About Michael

The person across from you matters.

I am the sole clinician at MindFull Counseling & Consulting. When you reach out, you reach me—and when you begin therapy, you work directly with me.

I am a Licensed Professional Counselor in Pennsylvania, seeing clients in person in Aston and by telehealth throughout Philadelphia and the rest of the state. I am also a Licensed Associate Counselor in New Jersey. My experience spans private practice, school counseling, and teaching psychology at the college level.

That breadth helps me see more than a set of symptoms. I consider the larger life around them: family, development, faith, identity, vocation, and the relationships that shape how you understand yourself.

Common questions.

Where is your office?

My office is at 5151 Chichester Avenue in Aston, PA 19014, in Delaware County. It is a short drive from Media, Brookhaven, Chester, Glen Mills, Garnet Valley, and Wallingford, with easy access from I-95 and Route 1.

Do you see clients from Philadelphia?

Yes. Many of my clients live in Philadelphia and meet with me by secure video, which saves the commute. Philadelphia clients who prefer in-person sessions are welcome at the Aston office, about a 30–40 minute drive from Center City.

Do you offer online therapy in Pennsylvania?

Yes. I provide telehealth counseling to adults, couples, and families anywhere in Pennsylvania through secure video.

Do you take insurance?

I am an out-of-network provider. I work with self-pay clients and some insurance options in Pennsylvania and New Jersey, and I can provide documentation you may be able to submit for out-of-network reimbursement. Reach out and I will walk you through your options.

How do I get started?

Email or call me with a few sentences about what brings you in. I respond personally, and we will set up a brief conversation to see whether I am the right fit.
